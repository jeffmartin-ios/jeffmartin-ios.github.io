Frequently Asked Questions

Which camera systems are supported?

Simulated Film supports custom recipe parameters for modern Fujifilm X-Series / GFX cameras and Ricoh GR cameras.

Is my recipe library private?

Yes. Recipes are stored locally on your device and synced privately via your personal iCloud account.

Can I import recipes from other photographers?

Yes, scan any Simulated Film QR code to add new looks straight into your collection.
