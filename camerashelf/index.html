Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my gear catalog stored?

All records are stored locally on your device for instant offline access, protected by automatic full-fidelity backups (.camerashelfbackup, CSV, ZIP, and PDF) that you can save to Apple Files, iCloud Drive, or external storage.

Does it support multiple currencies?

Yes. Record purchases in JPY, USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, CHF, and more while viewing normalized collection totals.

Can I export my gear list and backups?

Yes. Export clean PDF insurance manifests, production rental quotes, CSV spreadsheets, full ZIP photo archives, and portable .camerashelfbackup snapshots anytime.
