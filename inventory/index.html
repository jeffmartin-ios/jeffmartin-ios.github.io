Frequently Asked Questions

Can I export inventory reports and backups?

Yes. Export structured CSV spreadsheets and clean PDF inventory summaries anytime for accounting, audits, or team handoffs.

Does it sync across iPhone and iPad?

Yes. All records are stored privately in your personal iCloud account for seamless synchronization across your iPhone and iPad.

Does recording a sale update stock automatically?

Yes. Logging a sale automatically deducts item quantities, triggers low-stock alerts when needed, and updates your profit analytics.
