Frequently Asked Questions

Is my financial data private?

Yes. No bank account connections are required. All entries remain on your device and sync privately via your personal iCloud account.

What is included for free?

Daily expense tracking, Needs vs. Wants classification, and core daily budget limits are free to use, with optional Premium plans unlocking extended analytics and unlimited travel envelopes.

Can I export my transaction history?

Yes, you can search, filter by date, and export your full history or individual trip recaps to CSV anytime.
