Hey everyone! Over the past few weeks, we've been heads-down working on a massive overhaul for **Vocab Bento**. 

Between a complete visual redesign inspired by traditional Japanese stationery, an hourly Lock Screen Kanji widget, our new Shiba Inu mascot, and an entire second volume of themed lesson packs, there’s a lot to unpack. Here’s a quick tour of what’s new and why we built it.

---

### 📜 Handcrafted Washi & Ink Aesthetic

Standard digital flashcard apps can feel sterile. We wanted Vocab Bento to feel like pulling out a set of thick, tactile Torinoko paper cards at a quiet wooden desk in Kyoto.

- **6 Traditional Japanese Palettes:** Switch between *Washi & Sumi (和)*, *Sakura Blossom (桜)*, *Matcha & Bamboo (茶)*, *Aizome Indigo (藍)*, *Kyoto Wisteria (雅)*, and *Yuzu & Gold (柚)* with subtle ambient glows matched to your chosen palette.
- **Byōbu 3D Folding Animation:** Instead of flat "coin flip" card turns, tapping a card now folds it down the center crease like a traditional Japanese folding screen (*Byōbu / Orihon* 屏風・折本) before revealing the meaning.
- **Physical Deck Depth & Zen Dock:** Queued cards stack with slight organic offsets behind your active card, and a streamlined bottom dock lets you glide through reviews with one-tap actions (*Again*, *Reveal*, *Good*).

### 🏯 Hourly Lock Screen Kanji Widget

The easiest way to build vocabulary is through passive micro-exposures throughout the day. With our new Lock Screen widget, you get a fresh Kanji with Furigana readings and English translations right below your clock every hour. Tap it anytime to jump straight into a quick practice session.

### 🍱 Volume 2 Themed Lesson Packs

Passing the JLPT is great, but textbook Japanese doesn't always prepare you for ordering at a café, signing an apartment lease, or talking about music. 

We built **Volume 2** to focus specifically on real-life situational fluency:
- **Daily Living & Housing:** Leases, utilities, neighborhood rules, and household terms.
- **Shopping & Beauty:** Sizes, fabrics, discounts, skincare, and salon vocabulary.
- **Music & Entertainment:** J-Pop/Rock terms, live gigs, karaoke, and songwriting.
- **Sports & Fitness:** Martial arts (*Aikido, Kyudo, Iaido*), workouts, and baseball.
- **Onomatopoeia & Expressions:** Sound symbolism and mimetic expressions (*waku-waku*, *doki-doki*).
