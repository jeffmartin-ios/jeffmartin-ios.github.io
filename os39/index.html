Frequently Asked Questions

What audio formats and sources does OS-39 support?

OS-39 supports FLAC, ALAC (Apple Lossless), WAV, AAC, and MP3 via direct Files app linking as well as personal WebDAV, Navidrome, and Plex servers.

Can I stream from my own self-hosted music server?

Yes. Alongside local lossless files in the iOS Files app, OS-39 connects directly to your personal WebDAV, Navidrome, and Plex servers.

Is my listening history private?

Completely. All library indexing, EQ profiles, and listening recommendations run locally on your device with zero data collection.
