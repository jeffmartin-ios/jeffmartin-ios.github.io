Frequently Asked Questions

What crafts can I track?

Pattern Projects is tailored for sewing, knitting, crochet, embroidery, quilting, and general maker projects.

Is my project data private?

Yes. All project notes and photos stay on your device and sync privately through your personal iCloud account.

Can I catalog my material stash?

Yes, the dedicated Stash view keeps fabrics, yarns, and notions searchable alongside your active projects.
