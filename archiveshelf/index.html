Frequently Asked Questions

What can I track in ArchiveShelf?

Household electronics, appliances, collectibles, furniture, moving boxes, warranty expirations, serial numbers, and receipts across multiple currencies.

Can I export reports for insurance or moving?

Yes. ArchiveShelf generates structured PDF and CSV reports complete with item photos, valuations, and serial records.

Does ArchiveShelf collect my personal data?

Never. ArchiveShelf collects zero personal or behavioral data. Everything remains on your device and in your private iCloud storage.
