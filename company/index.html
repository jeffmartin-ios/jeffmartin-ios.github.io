Company Profile

Building enduring productivity software from Japan for the world.

Pandesal Bytes is a software studio that builds high-quality productivity apps for the Apple ecosystem. Primarily based in Japan and operating worldwide, we deliver focused, dependable software for individuals, creators, and businesses.
