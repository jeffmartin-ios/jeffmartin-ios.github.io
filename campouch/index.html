Frequently Asked Questions

Is CamPouch built for professionals or enthusiasts?

Both. CamPouch is intuitive enough for planning a weekend golden-hour portrait walk and comprehensive enough for commercial photographers managing studio setups and signed releases.

Is my client and project data private?

Yes. All project schedules, client details, signed PDFs, and locations are stored locally on your device and synced privately via your personal iCloud account.

Does the sun tracker work offline?

Yes. Core solar path, elevation, and golden-hour calculations run completely offline so you can plan reliably in remote outdoor locations.
