Tutorial

How to Add the Vocab Bento Widget on iOS 18 (Step-by-Step)

01

Wake your iPhone & Long-Press the Lock Screen

From your locked screen (unlocked with Face ID/Touch ID), press and hold on any empty area of the wallpaper until the wallpaper gallery appears. Tap the blue "Customize" button at the bottom, then choose Lock Screen.

02

Tap the Widget Box Beneath the Clock

Tap the rectangular widget strip located right below the clock display. The iOS 18 Widget Library tray will slide up from the bottom of your screen.

03

Select "Vocab Bento" & Choose Your Widget Size

Scroll through the app list to find Vocab Bento (勉強). You have two widget options:

  • Rectangular Widget (Recommended): Displays the large Kanji, Hiragana reading, English meaning, and stroke count.
  • Circular Widget: Displays the Kanji character with compact Onyomi/Kunyomi.
04

Customize Your Study Deck & Tap Done

Tap on the newly placed widget to select which JLPT level (N5, N4, N3, N2, or N1) or curated pack (Anime, Travel, Essential Verbs) you want it to draw from. Tap "Done" in the top right to save your new Lock Screen!
