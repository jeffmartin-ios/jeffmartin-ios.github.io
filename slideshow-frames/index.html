Frequently Asked Questions

What transition styles are included?

Slideshow Frames includes Cross Dissolve, Slide, Push, and Zoom transitions along with Vivid, Vintage, Sepia, and Monochrome looks.

What does Slideshow Frames Premium unlock?

Core slideshow creation and Home Screen widgets are free, while Premium unlocks unlimited albums, 4K Ultra HD video export, and the full collection of frame and transition styles.

Do my photos ever leave my iPhone?

Never. All rendering and video export run 100% locally on your device with zero cloud uploads or tracking.
