Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 20, 2025

Your Privacy is Fundamental to Our Architecture.

At Pandesal Bytes, we believe your personal and business data belongs exclusively to you. Every application we publish is engineered to be private by design, delivering dependable productivity tools without compromising customer confidentiality.

Data Collection and Storage

We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell personal information or behavioral usage data from our applications.

All records created in our applications are stored locally on your device and, where enabled, synchronized privately through Apple's iCloud service under your own Apple Account. Pandesal Bytes has no access to this data.

Third-Party Services & Analytics

Our applications contain no third-party advertising networks, cross-app tracking libraries, or external behavioral analytics SDKs.

Policy Updates

As our product lineup evolves, we may update this Privacy Policy to reflect new capabilities while maintaining strict transparency. Any updates will be published directly on this page.
